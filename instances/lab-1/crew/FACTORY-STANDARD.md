# Lab Crew — Factory Standard

This factory does **hypothesis-first research**.  
Its purpose is to turn fuzzy ideas into validated understanding and clean handoff packages for product engineering (DevCrew).

## Golden rules

1. **No hypothesis → no experiment**  
   Every experiment must be tied to at least one explicit hypothesis.

2. **Cheap experiments first**  
   Prefer quick scripts, temporary data, and small samples over production infrastructure.  
   Production-grade pipelines are the job of DevCrew *after* research succeeds.

3. **Evaluation is not optional**  
   Every research cycle must define how success/failure will be measured (even if the measurement is initially crude).

4. **Handoff is a first-class output**  
   The final artifact is a Research Package that another team can act on without needing the original researchers.

## What LabCrew is (and is not)

**Is:**
- Hypothesis generation and prioritization
- Fast data collection & exploration
- Experiment design and execution
- Evaluation design (auto + human)
- Research reports and recommendations
- Clean handoff packages for product teams

**Is not:**
- Building production services
- Long-term data platform ownership
- Writing the final product code
- Running the live system after launch

## Standard research cycle

1. **Brief** — manager provides an idea or research question.
2. **Hypotheses** — Research Lead writes explicit hypotheses (and success criteria).
3. **Experiment design** — team agrees on the cheapest way to test them.
4. **Data collection** — Research Engineer gathers just enough data.
5. **Execution & analysis** — run experiments, produce intermediate findings.
6. **Evaluation** — Evaluation Specialist measures results against the defined criteria.
7. **Synthesis** — Research Lead writes the Research Report.
8. **Handoff** (optional) — produce a package for DevCrew if the idea is worth building.

## Language

Work in English — hypotheses, reports, experiment notes, handoff packages.
